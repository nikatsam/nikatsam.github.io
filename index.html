TrayPilot

A compact Windows 11 flyout that keeps everyday controls close: adjust supported monitors, pick playback devices, watch live throughput and tweak common settings. You choose the shortcut that opens it.

Who it is for: For Windows 11 users who want display, audio and network controls one click away in the system tray.

What it does

  • Adjust brightness on supported monitors, with a laptop-panel fallback
  • Switch playback devices and mute speakers or microphones in one click
  • See download and upload speeds from active network adapters
  • Shortcuts for theme, Night Light, power mode, desktop icons and screen lock
  • Pick your own keyboard shortcut to bring up the panel

What it doesn't do

  • Require sign-in, cloud sync or usage tracking
  • Use the microphone or capture your screen
  • Run an always-on background service

ClerkBrain

ClerkBrain keeps an eye on the folders you pick, reads incoming paperwork (PDFs, Office files, photos and scans), pulls out the key figures and lets you search or ask questions about it. Naming and filing suggestions wait for your approval, and the on-device AI never sends your files elsewhere.

Who it is for: For anyone with piles of invoices and receipts who wants them sorted without uploading them anywhere.

What it does

  • Works through PDFs, Office files, photos and scans in the folders you select
  • Reads out supplier, date, total and invoice number
  • Finds files by keyword, or by meaning once the optional AI models are installed
  • Answers plain-language questions and points to the source documents
  • Suggests names and folders, with undo on every change
  • Marks uncertain readings and flags duplicates and leftovers
  • Builds a year-end pack for your accountant

What it doesn't do

  • Send your documents anywhere. The AI runs on your device
  • File anything without asking. Auto-filing stays off until you switch it on

Windows app · Live

Fix My Photo Library

Compare backups by content so renamed copies still count, remove exact duplicates with an undo option, flag damaged images and review camera date information. Changes are reversible and nothing is uploaded.

Who it is for: For anyone with a messy photo library full of duplicates who wants to clean it up safely.

What it does

  • Confirms backups by comparing contents, so renamed copies still match
  • Finds identical files and moves the extras into a reversible quarantine
  • Scans images for decoding faults, separating real damage from unsupported formats
  • Checks camera date and timezone details before touching timestamps
  • Restores a damaged photo from a healthy backup as a separate, verified copy

What it doesn't do

  • Rewrite EXIF metadata or invent missing timezone offsets
  • Repair damaged pixel data
  • Send your photos anywhere for processing

Windows app · Live

ShelfSearch

ShelfSearch builds a local index of filenames and folder paths from each drive you register. When a drive is offline you still see where a file lives, so you know which disk to plug back in.

Who it is for: For people whose files are spread across several drives and who need to find things fast.

What it does

  • Registers external and removable drives, mapped drives and chosen network shares
  • Lets you search while a drive is disconnected, and shows where it lives
  • Displays each drive's connection status, capacity and free space
  • Exports search results to a CSV file
  • Keeps the index on your own PC

What it doesn't do

  • Open or inspect the files on your drives
  • Send anything off the machine, or depend on a cloud service to work

Windows app · Live

What Was I Working On?

Recent files and folders are collected into a searchable history. A quick overlay or a full timeline lets you reopen the right item, and the record never leaves your machine.

Who it is for: For anyone who loses track of what they were working on and needs to get back to it quickly.

What it does

  • Opens a quick overlay of recent work from a shortcut
  • Browses a full timeline: today, yesterday, past 7 days and past 30 days
  • Filters by name, folder, app or file extension
  • Reopens files and folders straight from the timeline
  • Optional Windows 11 widget and reminders for supported apps
  • Scans known app recovery folders, only when you start it
  • Stores history and recovery copies encrypted

What it doesn't do

  • Need an account, cloud service, telemetry or internet access
  • Upload your history or document contents
