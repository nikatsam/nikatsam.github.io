Craft Fair Profit

Work out whether a craft fair is worth the effort before you book. Track stock, expenses and sales for each event, then review what to bring and what the day actually earned.

Who it is for: For craft sellers planning a fair who want to know whether it will be worth the cost.

What it does

  • Keep stock counts, costs and sales together for each event
  • Estimate break-even before you commit to a stall
  • Checklists for what to pack and what to record
  • Review each event afterwards to see what paid off
