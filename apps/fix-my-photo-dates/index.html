Fix My Photo Dates

When dates go wrong after an export or a move, this tool finds the mismatch, shows the proposed fix and lets you roll it back. Everything runs on your Windows PC.

Who it is for: For people whose photo dates went wrong after a Google Takeout export or moving files around.

What it does

  • Diagnoses and fixes timestamps after Takeout exports, iCloud downloads and file moves
  • Shows a preview before any change and lets you roll it back
  • All processing stays on your Windows PC
