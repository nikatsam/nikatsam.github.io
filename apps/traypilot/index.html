TrayPilot

A compact Windows 11 flyout that keeps everyday controls close: adjust supported monitors, pick playback devices, watch live throughput and tweak common settings. You choose the shortcut that opens it.

Who it is for: For Windows 11 users who want display, audio and network controls one click away in the system tray.

What it does

  • Adjust brightness on supported monitors, with a laptop-panel fallback
  • Switch playback devices and mute speakers or microphones in one click
  • See download and upload speeds from active network adapters
  • Shortcuts for theme, Night Light, power mode, desktop icons and screen lock
  • Pick your own keyboard shortcut to bring up the panel

What it doesn't do

  • Require sign-in, cloud sync or usage tracking
  • Use the microphone or capture your screen
  • Run an always-on background service
