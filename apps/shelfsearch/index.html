ShelfSearch

ShelfSearch builds a local index of filenames and folder paths from each drive you register. When a drive is offline you still see where a file lives, so you know which disk to plug back in.

Who it is for: For people whose files are spread across several drives and who need to find things fast.

What it does

  • Registers external and removable drives, mapped drives and chosen network shares
  • Lets you search while a drive is disconnected, and shows where it lives
  • Displays each drive's connection status, capacity and free space
  • Exports search results to a CSV file
  • Keeps the index on your own PC

What it doesn't do

  • Open or inspect the files on your drives
  • Send anything off the machine, or depend on a cloud service to work
