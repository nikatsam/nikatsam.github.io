Fix My Photo Library

Compare backups by content so renamed copies still count, remove exact duplicates with an undo option, flag damaged images and review camera date information. Changes are reversible and nothing is uploaded.

Who it is for: For anyone with a messy photo library full of duplicates who wants to clean it up safely.

What it does

  • Confirms backups by comparing contents, so renamed copies still match
  • Finds identical files and moves the extras into a reversible quarantine
  • Scans images for decoding faults, separating real damage from unsupported formats
  • Checks camera date and timezone details before touching timestamps
  • Restores a damaged photo from a healthy backup as a separate, verified copy

What it doesn't do

  • Rewrite EXIF metadata or invent missing timezone offsets
  • Repair damaged pixel data
  • Send your photos anywhere for processing
